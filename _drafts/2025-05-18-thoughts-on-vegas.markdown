Collette and I just returned from 3 nights in Las Vegas, Nevada. We had a great time, but we probably won't go back. The trip was planned around tickets to see Dead & Company at the Las Vegas Sphere, on May 15, 2025. The show was great, the Sphere was amazing, that experience alone would've made the trip worth it. 

We saw 3 other shows, all very good to excellent, toured the Mob Museum and took a half day trip to see Hoover Dam. We saw and did a lot in the just less than 3 days we were in  Vegas. Outside of the shows, which were costly but, in my opinion, worth it.

My overall impression of Las Vegas was that we were paying premium prices for an average to slightly below average experience. From waiting in line over 30 minutes just to check in, to the physical condition of the property - every time we looked we saw some ding or mark that should have been cleaned, repainted, or repaired, to the prices for everything, which we were expecting to be high but still managed to surprise us on the upside. Most everything, outside of the shows, was disappointing.

We stayed at Caesar's Palace which was pretty centrally located on the stip. Our room wasn't cheap. With all the fees we paid slightly over $400 per night, maybe that's cheap by 2025 Vegas standards, but we remember when Vegas was CHEAP, flights, hotel rooms, food. The conventional wisdom was they lured you to Vegas with cheap prices and made their profits from gambling
