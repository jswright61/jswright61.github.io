Small tools I've built for myself and use regularly. Each runs entirely in your browser: nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

## [Bag Tags](/bag_tags/)

Designs luggage tag inserts with your name, email, and phone numbers, and an optional QR code, then lays them out on a page as a PDF to print and cut. Templates and tag data are saved in your browser. [Privacy details](/bag_tags/privacy.html).

## [Password Generator](/pwgen/)

Makes memorable passwords from four or five random words, with optional random capitalization and separators. It shows the strength of each password and works offline. On a phone, add it to your home screen and it works like an app.
