My name is Scott Wright. I live in Smyrna (Atlanta), Georgia. My wife and I retired in 2023 after starting, running for 22 years, and selling a small business. These days I spend my time coding, biking, traveling, playing word games, and working as a poll worker at my local precinct.

I've been developing software and managing and tuning databases for 40 years — sometimes as a side job, sometimes full time, and now mostly as a hobbyist. I'm a long-time Deadhead, a Navy veteran and submariner, and a proud progressive. My pronouns are he/him.
